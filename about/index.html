Biography

Feiyu Zhou is a Ph.D. researcher at Xi'an Jiaotong University. His research focuses on AI for Science and computational materials, with particular interest in machine-learned interatomic potentials, uncertainty quantification, interfaces, irradiation damage, thermal transport, and electronic-structure response.

His work aims to connect first-principles accuracy with the scale and flexibility needed to study realistic materials processes beyond small equilibrium cells.
