01/Overview

Learning across atomic scales.

I develop machine-learning methods for atomistic simulation, materials modelling, and scientific discovery.

My work connects first-principles calculations with large-scale simulation, with a focus on machine-learned interatomic potentials, uncertainty-guided data acquisition, heterogeneous interfaces, irradiation damage, and transport physics.

I am especially interested in models that remain physically useful outside narrow equilibrium benchmarks: at interfaces, under strain, during defect evolution, and in non-equilibrium environments.
